Rules applied:
  • WD ≤ 7 → ₹0 payout (both metrics, even if tiers are met).
  • 8–15 WD → prorate each metric: (Metric Base Amount / 16) × WD.
  • WD ≥ 16 → full metric amount (no day proration).
  • Quality < Min Quality → ₹0 on both metrics.
Note: QVC payouts will be determined based on final unit counts, quality scores, and the number of working days. All figures are subject to verification and approval by the supervisory teams at the end of the month.
